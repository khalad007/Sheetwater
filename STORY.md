Sheetwater: locked story v1
Premise: Detective Mara Voss arrives in a village with no mirrors to find a missing 8-year-old, Elise. Villagers with blank faces send her to the school, the lake and the chapel, and at each place she finds one piece of the girl.
Truth: Elise is Mara’s daughter and drowned in the lake a year ago, during forty seconds of a work call. Mara has been “arriving” every day and forgetting each night.
The rule: Mara wrote “No mirrors. Don’t let me look.” in her own hand, after the first time she saw her face and walked into the lake. The villagers obey out of love, but they keep one covered mirror in the chapel because she has to choose to lift the sheet herself.
Faces: Mara can’t hold faces in memory, so everyone is blank to her and to the player. The faceless girl gains feet, coat, hands and voice as you find objects, and her eyes only appear in the mirror.
The unexplained detail: the lake is the only water that doesn’t reflect anything. It’s a pale, flat sheet.
Midpoint: under the inn bed is a pile of notebooks too big for the room, with entries like “Day 1. Found a boot.” and “Day 1. Found a boot, a coat.”
Endings: LOOK gives the girl a face, a reflection in the lake, and her voice one last time. LOOK AWAY brings the bus again and the pile grows, with small clues on replay. Neither is “the bad ending”, since one is grief faced and the other is another day of safety.
Fair clues: villagers always say “she” and never “Elise”. The case file has no photo and no reporting party. There’s a pale ring on Mara’s finger and an always-empty seat beside her on the bus. The voice recording cuts at one word.
Content note for itch.io: a child’s death and a suicide attempt.
